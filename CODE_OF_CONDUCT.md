# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in AI Interfaces welcoming, respectful, and
harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or level
of technical expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code constructively and assume good intent.
- Give specific, actionable feedback and accept feedback professionally.
- Respect privacy, security boundaries, and responsible-disclosure requirements.
- Credit other people's work and disclose relevant conflicts of interest.
- Focus disagreement on technical decisions, never on individuals.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sustained disruption, doxxing, sexualized conduct,
and publishing another person's private information are not tolerated. Deliberately
weakening security controls or publicly disclosing an unpatched vulnerability is also
unacceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

For ordinary conduct concerns, contact the maintainer privately through the contact
information on their GitHub profile. Report security vulnerabilities using the process
in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not through a public issue.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other participation
that violates this policy. Depending on severity and history, consequences may include
a warning, temporary restriction, or permanent removal from project spaces.

This policy applies in all project spaces and when someone officially represents the
project elsewhere.
